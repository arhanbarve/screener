## Process failure

**Six orders were executed and the reasoning was nearly lost.** The session
crashed between filling and journaling, and because `run_trader.sh` stamped the
day before starting, nothing retried and nothing flagged it. For 24 hours the
account held two brand-new positions (MYE, GTX) and a $15,000 SPY allocation with
no written justification anywhere.

Only `claude-mem`'s incidental capture made this entry possible — that is luck,
not a process. The durable fixes went in on 2026-08-04 (`824c0ed`): the session
runs under `caffeinate`, the stamp lands after the session, a crash retries only
when the book is verifiably untouched, and the Paper page now reports per-day run
cadence so a lost day is visible the same afternoon.

The remaining gap is that **journaling happens last.** A session that trades and
then dies still loses its record. Worth considering: write the decision rationale
*before* placing orders, or checkpoint it immediately after each fill.
