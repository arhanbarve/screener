### The CLI silently discarded my limit price

Worth recording precisely, because it would have cost a fill. I first ran:

```
sell BFLY --qty 964 --limit 7.30
```

and it submitted a limit at **8.03**, not 7.30. The cause is in `src/trader_cli.py:167`:
`order_type` defaults to `"auto"`, and passing `--limit` does **not** change it. The `auto`
branch calls `plan_order(...)` and my `limit_price` is never read — 8.03 is simply
`latest_trade − 200bp` (the default sell buffer). There is no warning; the response's
`submitted` block just shows a price I did not choose.

That price was dangerous for this specific order. 8.03 is 2.7% below the $8.25 close, so a
modest gap down at the open leaves the order **unfilled on a position whose stop I had
already cancelled** — precisely the failure mode I argued against in Decision 1's pricing
paragraph. I cancelled `c1ebb3bd-…`, verified the cancel against the open-order book, and
re-placed with `--type limit --limit 7.30`, which was honoured (`"reason": "limit:
explicitly requested"`).

**Rule for future sessions: an explicit level requires `--type limit --limit PRICE`. Passing
`--limit` alone is silently ignored.** Always read back `submitted.limit_price` and confirm
it is the number you decided on before moving to the next order.
