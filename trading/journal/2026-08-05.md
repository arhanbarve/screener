### New constraint discovered tonight, worth carrying forward

**Alpaca will not let a position simultaneously hold a resting protective sell stop
and a resting opposite-side buy order.** This bit twice in one session:

1. The first `buy VSXY` and `buy STX` attempts were **rejected outright** —
   `"reject_reason":"opposite side market/stop order exists"` — because their GTC
   stops were resting. Adding to a stopped position required `cancel-stops TICKER`
   first. ORKA and ARWR went through immediately, being new positions with no stop.
2. Having cancelled those stops to place the adds, the adds then blocked the stops
   from being restored.

So on any evening where the session **adds to an existing position**, that position
is necessarily unprotected until its add resolves. The documented procedure —
cancel one stop at the point of sale, re-sync at the end — silently assumed sells;
it does not hold for adds. `sync-stops` also **aborts the whole run on the first
rejection**, so STX's and VSXY's stops were never even attempted after SPY's failed;
it should collect rejections and continue instead of failing fast.

**Accepted rather than unwound.** The alternative was cancelling the three buy
limits to restore three stops — abandoning the deployment decision, which is the
entire point of this session, to guard a one-day gap on the index ETF and the two
strongest names in the book. That is precisely the 08-04 error in reverse: letting
a mechanism veto sound reasoning. The exposure window is one trading day, nothing
can move until 09:30, and tomorrow's session re-syncs once the adds resolve. The
honest residual risk is a *crashed* session tomorrow leaving SPY, STX and VSXY
floorless for longer — and with four of the first nine sessions having crashed,
that is not hypothetical.
