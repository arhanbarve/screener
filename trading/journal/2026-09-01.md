## Execution failure — what happened and what the next session must fix

**The plan did not execute.** Both substantive orders were rejected by Alpaca:

- `sell BFLY --qty 964 --limit 7.00` → **403 / 40310000**, `"insufficient qty available
  for order (requested: 964, available: 0.551802449)"`, `"held_for_orders": "964"`,
  related order `56a1792c-…`. The stop I cancelled is still holding the shares because
  the cancel has not processed.
- `buy SPY --qty 13 --limit 780.00` → **403 / 40310000**, `"potential wash trade
  detected. use complex orders"`, `"reject_reason": "opposite side market/stop order
  exists"`, existing order `daebbf21-…` (the SPY stop, also stuck cancelling).

**Root cause: Alpaca is queueing cancel requests while still accepting new orders.** Both
`cancel-stops` calls returned `"cancelled": [{}]` — an empty object, not an error — and
both orders went to `status: pending_cancel` and stayed there for ~15 minutes across
roughly a dozen polls. Meanwhile a brand-new order (the ORKA dust stop, `9e4fb997-…`) was
accepted instantly at 20:11 ET. So order *entry* is live and only *cancellation* is
queued.

The timing is the discriminator. Friday's identical cancel-then-sell sequence worked: order
`6984dff1-…` was created 16:34:49Z and cancelled 16:35:21Z — **32 seconds**, submitted at
16:35 ET. Tonight's cancels went in at **20:05–20:09 ET, past the 20:00 ET extended-session
close**, and have not resolved. They will almost certainly clear when the pre-market
session opens (~04:00 ET), long before the 09:30 open — but by then no session is running
to place the orders the cancels were meant to enable.

**Resulting state at the 09-01 open — this is the part that matters:**

Once the queued cancels clear overnight, **BFLY (~$7,580) and SPY (~$23,157) will have no
protective floor and no resting order.** I removed two stops and was unable to place
anything in their stead. That is a worse book than if I had done nothing, and it is my
error, not the broker's.

I judge the one-day risk tolerable rather than urgent: SPY's floor sat 3.1% below a broad
index, and a stop does not protect against a gap anyway; BFLY is a position I have decided
to exit, so a missing floor on it costs at most one day of drift on 8% of equity. But it
must be repaired first thing.

**Next session, before anything else:**

1. Run `sync-stops --apply` immediately to restore the BFLY and SPY floors (the shares
   will have freed up once the cancels clear).
2. Re-decide Decisions 1 and 2 on fresh prices — do **not** simply re-submit tonight's
   levels. BFLY at a $7.00 floor and SPY capped at $780 were priced off an 08-31 close
   that will be a day stale.
3. Confirm the two `pending_cancel` orders actually reached `canceled` and did not
   resurrect.

**Operational lesson — this changes the running order of the session.** The procedure's
step 0 says "Nothing to clear up front. Protective stops stay resting while you research."
Tonight shows that is wrong whenever the plan involves selling a position that has a stop:
the cancel is the long-latency step, and it has a hard cutoff at 20:00 ET. This session
started at 17:29 ET with plenty of margin and still missed the window, because research and
journaling consumed 2.5 hours and execution did not begin until 20:05.

The fix is not "work faster" — it is to **submit the cancel for any stop you might need
out of the way as the first action of the session, then research while it clears.** A
cancel is cheap and reversible: if the plan changes, `sync-stops --apply` puts the floor
straight back. Holding the cancel until after the analysis converts a reversible
precaution into a hard dependency on the broker's queue at the worst hour of the evening.
