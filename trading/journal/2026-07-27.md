2026-08-04 entry and commit `824c0ed`.

## Process note

This is the first of four trading days lost to the same fault: 07-27, 07-30,
08-03 (trades filled, journal lost) and 08-04. Because `run_trader.sh` stamped
the date *before* the session, a crash meant the day was never retried and the
gap was invisible. Both were fixed on 2026-08-04 — the stamp now lands after the
session, retry is gated on whether the book was touched, the session runs under
`caffeinate`, and the Paper page reports per-day run cadence.
