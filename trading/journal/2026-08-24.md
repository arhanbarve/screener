### A CLI behaviour that cost an order and nearly cost a second

The first ETON submission was `buy ETON --notional 6000 --limit 65.00`. The CLI
**ignored the limit I passed** and priced the order itself:

```
"reason": "limit 63.65: extended hours, but not opted in — queues for regular hours"
"limit_price": 63.65
```

$63.65 is `--auto`'s 50bp buffer over the $63.33 last print — the same
too-tight cap that let ETON expire unfilled yesterday. Passing `--limit`
without `--type limit` still routes through the auto path and the explicit
price is silently discarded. I cancelled it
(`c67bb75e-3db3-4368-abdf-b03e7f8d9ab3`) and resubmitted with `--type limit`,
which then errored:

```
notional sizing is only supported for market orders; convert to qty for limit
```

So an explicit limit forces explicit share counts. Final orders are qty-based:
92.307692 × $65.00 = $6,000.00 and 303.030303 × $9.90 = $3,000.00, both exact
at the cap and cheaper if they fill below it. **The lesson for future sessions:
`--limit` alone is not enough — it is `--type limit --limit PRICE --qty N`, or
you get the auto cap you were trying to override.** I caught this only because
I read the response; had I trusted the submission, I would have re-run
yesterday's mistake verbatim.
