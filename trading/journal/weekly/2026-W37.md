## Engine lesson → `config.yaml` proposal

**The core symbol loses its protective stop whenever a core leg is resting, and the engine
does not treat that as a cost.** Alpaca refuses a sell stop on a symbol that has an
opposite-side resting order (`40310000: potential wash trade detected`). This has now bitten
on 09-02, 09-03, 09-04 and again tonight on 09-14 — four times in eight sessions. Tonight it
left **$48.1k, 50.3% of equity, with no floor under it across a three-day weekend.** The
engine's `cancel_stop_first: true` on L4 made it worse: L4 was a *buy*, the stop was cancelled
to let it through, and then the resting buy blocked the stop from going back on.

This is not a bug in `sync-stops` — it correctly tried and correctly reported the failure. It
is a missing policy: the engine sizes core legs as though the stop is always restorable, and
it is not.

```yaml
trader:
  # A resting order blocks that symbol's protective stop (Alpaca wash-trade guard),
  # so any symbol with a live plan leg is unprotected until the leg fills. Refuse to
  # leave more than this fraction of equity stop-less; above it, split the core leg
  # into regular-hours slices so the gap is intraday rather than overnight.
  unprotected_exposure_cap: 0.25
```

Operationally, and independent of the config change: **`sync-stops --apply` must be re-run
after the open once resting legs fill**, not only in the evening session. Every one of the
four failures above would have been closed within minutes by that one habit.
