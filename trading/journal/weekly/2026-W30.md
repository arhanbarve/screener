## Lesson

The interactive session runs with `TRADER_DRY_RUN=1` baked into the shell
profile, and the permission layer blocks overriding it — so a manually
invoked session can validate orders but cannot submit live. Live paper
trading is expected to flow through the scheduled `run_trader.sh` path
(clean env). Need to confirm with the operator whether manual sessions
should ever trade live, or stay dry-run rehearsals feeding the scheduler.
