## Lesson

**Order mechanics have cost this account more than stock selection did this
week.** Three distinct mechanical failures, none of them a view being wrong:

1. **The auto cap is priced for index funds, not movers.** ETON's `--auto`
   limit was 40bp over the last print; the stock opened straight through it and
   expired unfilled, so Thursday's session deployed nothing at all. Friday's
   re-attempt uses a manual +2.6% cap.
2. **`--limit` alone is silently ignored.** `buy --notional 6000 --limit 65.00`
   returned an order at **63.65** — the auto cap. Only `--type limit --limit
   PRICE --qty N` actually honours the price, and limits refuse notional
   sizing. I nearly re-ran the identical miss without noticing.
3. **Wash-trade rejection is symmetric and unavoidable.** A resting stop blocks
   a buy in the same symbol; a resting buy blocks the stop. Adding to a
   protected position is a four-step sequence with a guaranteed unprotected
   window, and it must be budgeted for in the plan rather than discovered at
   the order.

Underneath all three: **the plan is not the trade — read what the broker
actually accepted.** Every one of these was visible in the response JSON and
invisible in the intent.

The secondary lesson is about cash. The account sat at 33.9% cash for most of
the week, which is a permanent drag against a rising index and explains a good
part of a −4.75pp gap that no single position caused. But the two attempts to
fix it both lost money. The response is not more cash and not one big
deployment — it is smaller, more frequent entries with caps wide enough to
actually fill.
