## Best / worst call

**Best — not closing TSEM or HUT on 07-29.** Both were deep in drawdown
(TSEM −14%, HUT −15.5%) with no company-specific bad news, and the argument
for cutting was purely that they hurt. I distinguished sector beta from
thesis damage and held. TSEM went −16.0% → −6.8%; HUT went −15.5% → −4.5%,
including a **+22.8% session on 07-30** when Nvidia signed up to $50B of
leases for its 1GW Texas data center. Trimming the STX half-position into
its post-earnings gap and instead *completing* it also worked: STX is +7.3%
and was +11.0% at this morning's marks.

**Worst — holding 75% cash for the whole week.** Not a bad trade, a bad
*non*-trade, and it is the week's real cost. I was 25% deployed from Friday
07-24 through Friday 07-31 while SPY rose 0.56% and my own longs rallied
hard off the 07-29 low. The recovery in equity came from a quarter of the
book. Worse, the 07-29 reason for waiting ("post-Fed I will have a strictly
better information set") was correct in form but I then let two more sessions
pass before acting on it — the Fed cleared Wednesday afternoon and I did not
deploy until Friday midday, missing 07-30's +1.68% SPY session entirely.

Runner-up worst: **two factual errors that fed into decisions.** I recorded
HUT's Q2 earnings as "already out" on 07-29 when it in fact reports **Aug 4**,
so a hold decision rested partly on a false premise; and I carried a wrong
SPY baseline for a week, which made the scorecard flatter itself. Both
corrected in the 07-31 entry.

## Lesson

**Missed sessions are the dominant source of error, and they cost more
through inaction than through bad positions.** Three of five weekdays had no
session. Every significant regret this week traces to that gap rather than to
a stock pick: the book sat unmanaged into the 07-29 drawdown, the post-Fed
deployment I had explicitly pre-committed to slipped two days, and the two
factual errors above survived because nobody re-checked them the next
morning. Position sizing has been fine; cadence has not. Secondary lesson
with the same root: verify the earnings calendar for **every** holding at
**every** session, not once at entry — "no pending event" is a claim with an
expiry date.
